5 Google News scrapers on the same queries - full text, direct links, several countries in one run

Five Google News Actors from the Apify Store, the same two queries in English and Ukrainian, measured on 26 November 2026: direct publisher links, full article text, run time and what you pay for. On the same pages: 26 of 27 full texts against 18 of 20.

Getting headlines out of Google News is easy. Getting the article itself - the direct publisher link and the full text, ready for a summary, a digest or a RAG index - is where Google News tools differ. I ran the three most used Google News Actors in the Apify Store, plus memo23 and mine, on the same two queries and compared what came back. All numbers are from runs on 26 November 2026.

Disclosure: one of the five is mine, Google News Scraper. Every number below comes from a real run with the same queries and options for every Actor.

The test

  • Two queries: "artificial intelligence" in the US edition (US:en) and «штучний інтелект» in the Ukrainian one (UA:uk).
  • 10 articles from the last 7 days per query. Mine with sortBy: "relevance", the others in their default order.
  • Direct publisher links and full article text switched on wherever the Actor offers them.

No duplicates in any output. The one US page nobody could read, on war.gov, is closed to robots for every Actor - sites that block robots (Reuters is another) are a limit for all Google News tools, mine included.

Full text on the same pages

The most useful number is what each tool does with the same pages. data_xplorer and memo23 returned 27 different pages between them. On those 27 pages my tool extracted the full text from 26 (the same extraction code as in the Actor, run on the pages from their output); data_xplorer and memo23 got 18 of their 20 each.

On two of these pages a competitor came back empty and mine got the full text: gur.gov.ua (data_xplorer: 0 words, mine: 250) and dou.ua (memo23: 0 words, mine: 2,140). The whole Ukrainian run - 10 articles, every one with its direct link, image and full text - took 6.6 seconds.

Several countries in one run

All four other Actors take one region per run. Mine takes a list: UA:uk, UA:ru, US:en, DE:de, PL:pl and 40+ more, together with several queries. Comparing how one story is covered in three markets is one run, not three.

Your order of results

sortBy: "relevance" gives Google's own order - the best choice when you want the most complete texts. sortBy: "newest" puts today's articles first - the best choice for monitoring.

The page part is charged only when the page was actually read. A site that blocks robots or a paywall costs nothing beyond the headline. A fully read article costs $3 per 1,000 on Bronze - a fair price, with no hidden extras.
