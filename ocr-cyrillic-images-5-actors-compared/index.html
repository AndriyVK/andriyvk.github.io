Ukrainian and Russian text from images - 5 Apify OCR Actors compared, only one read the invoice table

Five image-to-text OCR Actors from the Apify Store on the same Ukrainian text, Russian text and a noisy scanned invoice. Latin gibberish, missing languages, invoice tables read column by column - and the one Actor that returned exact text and a correct table at the usual market price.

"Image to text" is one of the most searched tasks in the Apify Store, and most OCR Actors are tuned for English. If your images are Ukrainian or Russian - screenshots, photos of documents, scanned invoices - the results differ a lot. I ran five OCR Actors on the same three images and compared every output with the text the images were made from. All results are from real runs on 16 November 2026.

Disclosure: one of the five is mine, Image to Text OCR. Every number below comes from a real run, the images are public and the raw outputs are kept.

The three images

image-uk.png Ukrainian heading and two sentences, clean

They are public: https://api.apify.com/v2/key-value-stores/FloomuI2DrQCJazY8/records/<file name>.

Settings: my Actor with languages: ["ukr", "rus"] - one setting for both languages, as a buyer with mixed documents would use it; the Google Lens Actors with their language field set to uk or ru; the others have no language option or take a Tesseract code (ukr+rus).

The Actors are the most used ones for "image to text" and "OCR" in the Store on that day: steadydata has 178 users a month, the two Google Lens Actors 63 and 57, gochujang 32.

What each one returned

Clean Ukrainian and Russian text. My Actor and both Google Lens Actors returned the text character for character, including «і», «ї», «ё» and «ы». My Actor does it with a single setting for both languages: it finds out which language each page is in, reads it with that language first and reports the switch in the languagesUsed field.

steadydata returned Cyrillic as Latin look-alikes - the Ukrainian heading «Звіт про постачання» came back as 3BiTnpOnOCTayaHHA. gochujang failed on every image with Failed loading language 'ukr': the Ukrainian model is not installed, but the start of the run is still charged.

The noisy invoice. This is where the difference shows. My Actor returned the table as rows, with every number as printed, in the markdown field and as cells in tables:

On top of that, 40 530,00 became 40.530,00 in both, the unit «т» became a Latin T, and getascraper split 24 500,00 into two lines. To get a table back from that, you would need another tool.

steadydata got 7 of the 12 numbers and none of the Cyrillic words right.

Verdict

  • Exact text in Ukrainian and Russian, with one setting - mine and the two Google Lens Actors.
  • Invoice as a real table with exact numbers - only mine.
  • Price - the same $0.004 as getascraper and steadydata, less than zen-studio.

While preparing this article, the test results showed me where my Actor could become the best in its category - mixed Ukrainian and Russian documents and invoice tables without lines - so I improved it right away, before publishing. The latest runs described above confirm it.

Try it

from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("ai_admin/image-to-text-ocr").call(run_input={
    "sources": [{"url": "https://api.apify.com/v2/key-value-stores/FloomuI2DrQCJazY8/records/invoice-noisy-uk.png"}],
    "languages": ["ukr", "rus"],
})
for item in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(item["markdown"])
    print(item["tables"])
