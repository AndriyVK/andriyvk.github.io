Monitor Ukrainian and Russian news with Google News - direct links, full article text and several countries in one run

Get Google News articles in Ukrainian, Russian and English as clean JSON in one run - direct publisher links, images and full article text for AI and RAG. A real run, the output, and how the price and features compare with the six most used Google News Actors.

Media monitoring, brand mentions, a morning digest for a team, a news feed for an AI assistant - all of these start with the same step: get the articles out of Google News as data. For Ukrainian and Russian sources there is one more twist: the same story is covered in both languages, so you want both editions at once. This post shows a tool that does that, with a real run from 16 November 2026.

In short: the most complete news data in one run. Direct publisher links, full article text and several countries at once - all in one article price, with no extra fees per link, per page or per run.

Disclosure: I built the tool, Google News Scraper on Apify. The code is open: AndriyVK/-apify-document-tools.

One run, two languages

from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("ai_admin/google-news-scraper").call(run_input={
    "queries": ["Укренерго"],
    "editions": ["UA:uk", "UA:ru"],
    "timeframe": "1d",
    "maxArticlesPerQuery": 10,
    "articleDetails": True,
    "fullText": True,
})
for item in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(item["edition"], item["source"], item["url"])

The run took 9 seconds and returned 19 articles: 10 from the Ukrainian edition and 9 from the Russian one, with no duplicates. Every one of them came with:

  • the direct link to the article on the publisher's site (19 of 19), not a Google News redirect;
  • the article image and description (19 of 19);
  • the full article text, cleaned of menus and ads, with a word count - ready to summarise or to put into a vector database. Four of the articles were from Ukr.net, which publishes short summaries of 24-28 words, and they came back as such.

What else it does

  • Several queries and editions in one run: UA:uk, UA:ru, US:en, DE:de, PL:pl and 40+ more. Compare how one story is covered in each market without running the tool five times.
  • More than 100 articles per query. Google News shows at most 100 results per search. Give a date range and the tool reads it day by day, up to 5,000 articles per query.
  • Search operators as on Google News: "exact phrase", OR, -exclude, site:, intitle:.
  • Topic headlines (TOP, WORLD, BUSINESS, TECHNOLOGY and others), often with the other outlets that cover the same story.
  • No proxy and no API key needed.

Ideas for using it

  • Morning digest. Schedule the run every day at 7:00 with your brand, competitors or a topic, then send the new articles to a team channel or let an AI write a summary from the text field.
  • Ukrainian and Russian coverage side by side. One query, editions UA:uk and UA:ru, and you see who writes what.
  • A news dataset for RAG. Topic headlines for a month with full text, one record per article, no duplicates.
