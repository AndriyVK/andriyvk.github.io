Transcribe audio and podcasts by API - word timestamps, translation into 21 languages and a free summary

One API call turns MP3, WAV, M4A or video files and podcast RSS feeds into text, timed segments, SRT/VTT subtitles, every word with its time, a translation and a summary with action items. Real runs in Ukrainian and English, and what each option costs.

A meeting recording, an interview, a podcast episode, a lecture - most of the time you need more than the words. You need the words with their times, subtitles, a translation for a colleague and a short summary of what was decided. This post shows a tool that returns all of that from one call. Every output below is from real runs on 26 November 2026.

Disclosure: I built the tool, Audio & Video Transcriber on Apify. The code is open: AndriyVK/-apify-document-tools.

One call

from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>")
run = client.actor("ai_admin/audio-transcriber").call(run_input={
    "sources": [{"url": "https://api.apify.com/v2/key-value-stores/FloomuI2DrQCJazY8/records/speech-en.mp3"}],
    "podcastFeeds": ["https://api.apify.com/v2/key-value-stores/FloomuI2DrQCJazY8/records/test-podcast.xml"],
    "maxEpisodesPerFeed": 2,
    "translateTo": "uk",
    "wordTimestamps": True,
    "summarize": True,
})
for item in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(item["language"], item["text"])
    print(item["summary"]["actionItems"])

The run had two files and a podcast feed with two episodes, in English and Ukrainian, and took 22 seconds. The language of each file was detected automatically.

What came back

The transcript, as text and as timed segments, plus SRT and VTT subtitle files:

0.0-6.0    The warehouse in Lviv received 1,240 pallets of building materials during the third quarter.
6.0-10.7   Delivery times improved by 12% after the new routing schedule was introduced.

What it does

  • Whisper large-v3, 99 languages including Ukrainian; quality: "fast" switches to large-v3-turbo at the same price.
  • Audio and video in: MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, FLAC, MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI. Long files are split automatically.
  • Podcasts by RSS: give the feed, and the newest episodes are transcribed with their titles and dates.
  • Free extras: SRT and VTT subtitles, every word with its time, and the summary with key points and action items.
  • Translation into 21 languages, transcript and subtitles. If the audio is already in that language, nothing is translated and nothing is charged - the output says so in a note.
  • Your own terms: vocabulary takes names and spellings that appear in the recording.
  • Cross-check for noisy audio: the recording is recognised again by the second model and the places where the two disagree are listed with their times.
  • Failed files are free.
